Roles and Responsibilities

This document defines the different roles involved in contributing, reviewing, and maintaining administrative boundary data.

It clarifies expectations for both external contributors and internal team members.

External Perspective

If you would like to submit a boundary layer, here is what we expect:

  • The boundary represents the country as it represents itself.
  • The correct ADM level is identified.
  • The projection and geometry meet our standards.
  • Required metadata is included.
  • Files are properly zipped and named.
  • The submission follows the Quality Gates checklist.

Submissions that do not meet minimum standards may be returned for revision.

Role Definitions

There are three primary roles in the boundary workflow:

  1. Contributor
  2. Researcher
  3. Curator

Each role has different responsibilities and levels of involvement.

1. Contributor

A Contributor submits a boundary dataset for consideration.

Typical Characteristics

  • May be a student, or external user.
  • Submits a shapefile through:
  • The project website submission portal.
  • Emailing directly team@geoBoundaries.org

Responsibilities

  • Submit boundary files in the correct format.
  • Follow naming conventions.
  • Provide required metadata.
  • Ensure geometry validity.
  • Package files correctly into a zip file.

2. Researcher

A Researcher conducts in-depth boundary research before submission.

Typical Characteristics

  • Works on a boundary for an extended period (weeks to months).
  • Triangulates multiple references.
  • Digitizes boundaries when needed.

Responsibilities

  • Locate and validate boundary sources.
  • Reproject and standardize files.
  • Repackage data into the required schema.
  • Upload finalized files into the repository.

Researchers are expected to meet full technical and documentation standards before submission.

3. Curator

A Curator performs final oversight and approval. This role is an internal position of oversight at William and Mary.

Responsibilities

  • Review submissions for compliance with Quality Gates.
  • Validate geometry and projection.
  • Confirm naming and schema compliance.
  • Assess whether the boundary reflects official national definitions.
  • Approve, request revisions, or reject submissions.
  • Maintain consistency across the repository.

Curators ensure the integrity and long-term quality of the dataset.

Internal Roles (William & Mary)

Within William & Mary:

  • Curators manage dataset integrity and support quality control.

These roles maintain institutional oversight of the dataset.

External Roles

External participants typically fall into two categories:

  • Contributor
  • Researcher

External participants do not perform final approval but are essential to expanding and improving coverage.

Workflow Overview by Role

Role Research Required Technical Standard Final Approval Authority
Contributor Low–Moderate Required No
Researcher High Required No
Curator Moderate Enforced Yes

Summary

  • Contributors submit data.
  • Researchers submit, create, validate, and standardize data.
  • Curators ensure integrity and approve inclusion.

Each role plays a critical part in maintaining quality, consistency, and trust in the boundary dataset.
